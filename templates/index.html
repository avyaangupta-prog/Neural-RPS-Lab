THE IMPORTANT ML LESSON

Patterns are learnable. True randomness is not.

The network is trained on synthetic opponents that repeat, bias, cycle, alternate, or follow transition patterns. A random player has no reliably predictable next move, so do not treat confidence as proof. This demo predicts the next move using prior rounds only; the move you are about to make is never leaked into that round's prediction.
